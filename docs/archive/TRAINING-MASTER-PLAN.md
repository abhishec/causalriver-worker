## Quality Metrics Per Training Pack

Every pack MUST include:
1. **4-6 causal chains** with effect sizes, lag days, and p-values
2. **1-2 business rules** with condition logic and action triggers
3. **1-2 cascades** mapping cross-domain ripple effects
4. **2-4 patterns** with observed/expected statistical evidence
5. **2-3 outcomes** for prediction calibration (including 1 false)
6. **Narrative** explaining the full cascade in plain English
7. **Source citation** — real research, not made up
8. **Confidence score** — honest assessment (0.75-0.90 range)

## How Knowledge Flows Through the 7 Layers

```
Layer 1: SIGNALS (cross_domain_signals)
  └─ 6 live APIs pump 699 signals every 6 hours
  └─ Each signal: domain, type, value, entity, metadata

Layer 2: CAUSAL EDGES (causal_relationships_statistical)
  └─ 159 edges connecting domains with effect sizes & lag times
  └─ Trained from pack causal chains + discovered by learner

Layer 3: PATTERNS (ai_memory type='insight')
  └─ 92 statistical patterns (observed vs expected)
  └─ Chi-squared test validates significance

Layer 4: RULES (ai_memory type='rule')
  └─ 44 business rules with conditions → actions
  └─ Auto-activate when confidence > 0.7

Layer 5: CASCADES (org_cascade_rules)
  └─ ~60 cross-domain cascade rules
  └─ Trigger alerts when domain events ripple

Layer 6: PREDICTIONS (prediction_records)
  └─ ~60 prediction records tracking accuracy
  └─ Calibrates confidence over time

Layer 7: AUTONOMOUS LEARNING (activates at 30+ days)
  └─ Granger causality discovery on time-series signals
  └─ Auto-generates new training packs
  └─ Self-corrects inaccurate predictions
  └─ STATUS: Waiting for 30 days of signal accumulation (~29 days to go)
```

## Competition Differentiators

What makes NexusBrain unique vs other AI/BI tools:

1. **Causal, not correlational** — We encode WHY things happen, not just THAT they happen
2. **Cross-domain cascades** — A rate hike (finance) → hiring freeze (people) → velocity drop (engineering) → churn (cs) → revenue miss (finance)
3. **Self-learning** — Layer 7 discovers NEW causal relationships automatically
4. **Time-lagged** — Every relationship has a specific lag (30, 60, 90 days), enabling PREDICTION
5. **Evidence-weighted** — Confidence scores calibrate with real outcomes
6. **Multi-industry** — Works across SaaS, healthcare, fintech, etc.
7. **Free data** — No paid APIs required, all public research
